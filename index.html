About

Affordable care, backed by Pneuma Health.

My Safe Zone is an affordable therapy initiative by Pneuma Health. It was created to make professional mental health support easier to understand, easier to book, and more financially accessible.

Care may be provided by Registered Psychotherapists (RPs), Registered Psychotherapists (Qualifying), and Registered Social Workers (RSWs), depending on your needs and availability.

Insurance

Coverage depends on your plan.

Many extended-health plans include psychotherapy or social-work benefits. We can bill some insurers directly; with other plans, you pay for the session and submit our receipt for reimbursement.

Please confirm that your plan covers your clinician's professional designation before booking. We are happy to help you identify what to ask your insurer.
