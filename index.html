Before your first appointment

Affordable online therapy: your questions answered.

Read our guide to finding affordable therapy in Ontario →

My Safe Zone is the affordable therapy program of Pneuma Health, based in Ontario, Canada. Start with a therapist match or choose a clinician and time directly through our online booking page.

How much does a therapy session cost?

My Safe Zone sessions cost $99 CAD for 60 minutes. You can view therapists and current appointment availability in Jane.

Can I enquire from anywhere in Canada?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from across Canada, not only Ontario. Before you book, our team will confirm a suitable therapist’s availability and eligibility to provide care in the province or territory where you will be physically located during sessions. Contact us for help finding a therapist for your location.

What can I talk to a therapist about?

Our matching tool includes anxiety, depression and low mood, stress, relationships, trauma, grief, life changes and newcomer experiences. Each therapist has different areas of experience. Review their profile and discuss your needs with them to decide whether the fit is right for you.

Who provides the therapy?

Care may be provided by Registered Psychotherapists, Registered Psychotherapists (Qualifying), or Registered Social Workers, depending on your needs and availability. Read each clinician’s profile in Jane for their designation, approach and areas of experience.

What happens in the first session?

Your first session is a chance to get to know your therapist, talk about what brings you to therapy, and discuss what you would like support with. Your therapist will explain consent, confidentiality and its limits, and how you might work together. You can ask questions and share at your own pace; you do not need to prepare a perfect explanation or tell everything at once.

How do I choose a therapist and see available times?

Open our therapist and appointment page to read profiles and see current availability. You can also use the matching questions on this page, or ask our team for help choosing. On a phone, Jane may show only two days at a time, starting at the beginning of the week. Use the calendar arrows or date picker to see upcoming days. If you cannot find a suitable time, ask our team for help.

Does insurance cover the $99 session?

Your plan may cover psychotherapy or social work services, depending on the benefits included and your chosen therapist’s professional designation. Before booking, ask your insurer whether sessions with that specific therapist at Pneuma Health / My Safe Zone are eligible, and check any referral requirements, annual limits or per-session limits. Our team can help you find the therapist’s name, designation and registration number for this check. Receipts are issued under Pneuma Health; reimbursement is not guaranteed.

Can you bill my insurance directly?

Direct billing may be available for some plans and therapists. Contact our team to check before your appointment. If direct billing is unavailable, you can use your receipt to submit a claim to your insurer. You remain responsible for fees your plan does not cover.

How are online card payments secured?

Online card payments are handled through Jane Payments, our booking platform’s payment service. Jane Payments is PCI-compliant and uses Stripe to process card payments. Please enter payment details only through the secure Jane payment flow, rather than sending them by email or through our matching form. Read about Jane’s payment security.

What if the suggested therapists do not feel right?

You can try our therapist matching questions or request help from our team so our team can help you explore other options. Matching suggestions are a starting point, not a clinical assessment.

Are in-person sessions available?

Sessions are primarily online. In-person appointments in Richmond Hill may be arranged on request, subject to availability. Contact our team to discuss arrangements.

About

Affordable care, backed by Pneuma Health.

My Safe Zone is the affordable care program of Pneuma Health. It was created to make professional mental health support easier to understand, easier to book, and more financially accessible. Therapy services, billing, insurance claims, and receipts are provided under Pneuma Health.

Care may be provided by Registered Psychotherapists (RPs), Registered Psychotherapists (Qualifying), and Registered Social Workers (RSWs), depending on your needs and availability.

Insurance

Coverage depends on your plan.

You may self-pay, submit a Pneuma Health receipt to your insurer, or request direct billing. Direct billing is not available for every insurer or plan and cannot be guaranteed.

Please confirm that your plan covers psychotherapy or your clinician's professional designation. You remain responsible for the full fee and any amount your insurer does not pay.
