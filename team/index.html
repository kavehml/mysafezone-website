Pablo Allen

Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying)

Languages: English, Spanish

Pablo Allen is a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) in Ontario with over 15 years of clinical experience in Gestalt Psychotherapy, working with children, adolescents, adults, parents, couples, and families. His humanistic, relational, and trauma-informed approach integrates attachment theory, emotional awareness, mindfulness, and experiential interventions to support individuals navigating anxiety, trauma, relationship difficulties, life transitions, and personal growth. Originally trained in Mexico and further trained in the United States, Pablo brings extensive experience in clinical practice, psychotherapy education, and work with culturally diverse populations. As an immigrant himself, he understands the challenges of cultural adaptation, identity, and belonging, and is committed to providing compassionate, culturally sensitive care in both English and Spanish.

Nicholas Bonk

Registered Psychotherapist

Languages: English

I am a registered psychotherapist, and offer sessions in English, either online, or over the phone. I help clients work through a variety of problems including stress, anxiety, depression, trauma, family issues, interpersonal issues, relationship issues, and daily difficulties. I offer a variety of counselling modalities including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Solution-Focused Therapy, Emotion Focused Therapy, and Dialectical Behavioral Therapy.

I offer a cooperative experience where the client has a say in the direction of counselling. Additionally, the goals of counselling are tailored to the client’s individual needs, comfort level, and perspective to promote personal growth, fulfilment, and a greater understanding of self.

Sessions are a safe setting to explore past, current, and future struggles. Both day-to-day difficulties, and major life events are explored in an environment of unconditional, nonjudgemental support.
