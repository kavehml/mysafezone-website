Meet Sana

Sana works with children, teens and adults, including newcomers. Her approach is collaborative and culturally responsive, and she uses cognitive behavioural therapy to support coping skills and personal goals.

You can bring questions, uncertainty and the experiences that matter to you. You and your therapist can discuss what support might be useful and whether working together feels right.
