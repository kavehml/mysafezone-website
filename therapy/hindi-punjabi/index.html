Meet Keerat

Keerat works with adults experiencing anxiety, depression, grief, relationship challenges and life changes. She brings experience in the social service sector and a particular interest in supporting newcomers and cultural transitions.

Her approach is warm and collaborative, with space to explore your experiences at your own pace. You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out.

Getting started

Review Keerat’s profile and current appointments in Jane. Confirm your preferred language when arranging your session. Sessions are primarily online; the booking calendar displays its selected time zone.

Questions before booking?

Sessions are $99 CAD for 60 minutes. Insurance reimbursement depends on your plan and the individual clinician’s designation; please confirm with your insurer. If you are outside Ontario, ask us to confirm an appropriate therapist for your location before booking.

Request help from our team or email care@mysafezone.ca. Please keep health details and payment information out of email.
