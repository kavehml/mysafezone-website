A little clarity before you begin

Questions are welcome.

Who is My Safe Zone for?

My Safe Zone welcomes anyone exploring affordable private-pay therapy, including people experiencing financial difficulties, newcomers, refugees and long-time community members. Community workers can share this page, and people are welcome to contact us directly. Our team can help identify a practitioner whose services fit their needs and location.

What if $99 is difficult for me?

Please speak with our team before deciding care is out of reach. Tell us that cost is a concern, and we can discuss the options available for your situation. The published therapy fee is $99 CAD for 60 minutes; any different arrangement must be agreed with the office before care begins.

Can insurance help with the cost?

Your plan may cover psychotherapy or social work, depending on your benefits and the individual practitioner’s designation. Ask your insurer about your chosen practitioner before booking. Receipts are issued under Pneuma Health. Our team can help you find the information needed to check coverage.

Do I need to know which therapist to choose?

No. Read profiles if you would like, or use our short contact request form. It asks for basic contact information and a preferred time to reach you; you do not need to explain your personal story. A contact request does not book a session.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions are primarily online. You can also ask our team about in-person appointments in Richmond Hill.
