A therapy resource for your community

My Safe Zone offers $99 CAD, 60-minute psychotherapy sessions with registered mental health professionals. Community organizations can share this resource with people exploring private-pay therapy.

Who we can help you connect with

Our team includes Registered Psychotherapists, Registered Psychotherapists (Qualifying) and Registered Social Workers. Each practitioner has their own scope, age groups and availability. We help people identify a suitable option without promising a particular outcome.

  • Arabic and English: Sana Choghri, children, teens and adults.
  • Hindi, Punjabi and English: Keerat Aujla, adults.
  • Explore other team members and their profiles.

Sharing the service

You may share this page or our booking links in a resource list, newsletter or a conversation with someone who is looking for therapy. People can contact us directly; please do not send their personal or health information without an appropriate consent and secure referral process.

Community information sessions

Organizations interested in a clinician-led educational session can contact our office to discuss the audience, topic and arrangements. Dates and clinician participation are confirmed individually. An information session is general education, not individual therapy.

Suggested resource-list wording

My Safe Zone, a service of Pneuma Health, offers primarily online psychotherapy at $99 CAD for a 60-minute session. View individual clinicians, languages and appointment times at mysafezone.ca. Insurance reimbursement varies by plan and practitioner.

Questions before booking?

Sessions are $99 CAD for 60 minutes. Insurance reimbursement depends on your plan and the individual clinician’s designation; please confirm with your insurer. If you are outside Ontario, ask us to confirm an appropriate therapist for your location before booking.

Request help from our team or email info@pneumahealth.ca. Please keep health details and payment information out of email.
