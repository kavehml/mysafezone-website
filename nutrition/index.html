Registered Dietitian (RD)

Farnoosh Fakoorziba is a Registered Dietitian and a member of the College of Dietitians of Ontario. She offers nutrition counselling and education in English and Farsi, with experience supporting clients across the lifespan.

Her compassionate, client-centred approach focuses on caring for the body and building a nourishing relationship with food. Farnoosh shares simple, practical guidance that clients can apply in everyday life.

Nutrition counselling: $99 CAD for 60 minutes or $60 CAD for 30 minutes.
